Candidate experience

What candidates actually see

The candidate experience is designed to feel respectful, transparent, and mobile-first — with clear AI disclosure, language choice, and a human fallback at every step.

👋

Welcome & context

The first message introduces the process — what it covers, how long it takes, and that they're speaking with an AI. Candidates know before they start.

"You're speaking with an AI assistant on behalf of [Company]. This takes about 8 minutes."
🌐

Language preference

Candidates choose their preferred language — English or Hindi — before answering a single question. No assumption, no friction.

💬

Structured conversation

Questions are role-specific and conversational — not a form. The AI listens, follows up on unclear answers, and keeps the exchange natural.

⏸️

Pause & reschedule

Candidates can pause at any point and resume later — on the same channel. If they need to reschedule an interview, they reply with a date and it's handled automatically.

👤

Human support option

At any point, candidates can request to speak with a human. The handoff is instant — with full conversation context passed to your recruiter.

Opt-out: candidates can withdraw their application and request data deletion at any time.
✅

Completion & next step

Candidates receive a confirmation of submission and a clear next step — what happens next, and when they'll hear back. No ghosting.
